# LinkedIn Opinion Post Template

Use this template to produce opinion-driven LinkedIn posts from distilled content.

---

## Metadata

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Source Framework** | {framework_name} |
| **4A Angle** | {angle} |
| **Author Voice** | {author_voice} |
| **Status** | {draft | review | ready | published} |
| **Created** | {date} |

---

## Hook

{hook_line}

{One provocative or contrarian opening line that stops the scroll. Should challenge a common belief or state a bold opinion. Max 2 lines before the "see more" fold.}

---

## Opinion Statement

{opinion_statement}

{Clear, unambiguous stance on the topic. 1-2 sentences. This is the core thesis of the post.}

---

## Evidence / Reasoning

{evidence_block}

{Support the opinion with 2-3 concrete points. Use one of these structures:}

{- Personal experience or observation}
{- Data point or statistic}
{- Framework or mental model reference}
{- Contrast with the conventional wisdom}

---

## Nuance / Caveat

{nuance_statement}

{Acknowledge the counterargument or limitation. This adds credibility. 1-2 sentences.}

---

## Takeaway

{takeaway_line}

{One actionable sentence the reader can apply immediately.}

---

## CTA

{cta_text}

{Call to action — question to spark discussion, invite to comment, or link to deeper resource.}

---

## Hashtags

{hashtags}

---

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Hook is under 2 lines (visible before "see more")
- [ ] Opinion is clear and specific (not vague)
- [ ] Evidence supports the opinion (not invented)
- [ ] Nuance prevents strawman criticism
- [ ] Takeaway is actionable
- [ ] CTA invites engagement
- [ ] Total length 800-1500 characters
- [ ] Source framework properly credited
- [ ] Standalone value (works without source context)
