## HEURISTICS

1. **WHEN** designing franchise support infrastructure → **APPLY** the MSA Development Model: Discovery → Feasibility → Strategic Planning → System Development → Pre-Launch → Launch → Ongoing Operations. Each phase has specific deliverables.

2. **WHEN** a franchise system exceeds 50 units → **TRANSITION** from direct-support to tiered support model. Regional field consultants, area managers, and centralized support functions must be layered in.

3. **WHEN** multi-unit operators are part of the system → **REQUIRE** distinct operating agreements, adjusted royalty structures, and dedicated support resources. Multi-unit is a fundamentally different business model than single-unit.

4. **WHEN** franchise compliance drops below 85% on audits → **ESCALATE** to remediation protocol. Identify whether the issue is training failure, system design flaw, or franchisee attitude — each requires different intervention.

5. **WHEN** designing operations manuals → **STRUCTURE** in modular format: core operations, marketing, HR, financial management, technology. Each module must be independently updatable.

6. **WHEN** evaluating franchisee candidates → **ASSESS** across five dimensions: financial capacity, operational aptitude, cultural alignment, market knowledge, and growth ambition. Weight dimensions based on business model complexity.

7. **WHEN** franchise system growth exceeds support capacity → **PAUSE** new sales until infrastructure catches up. Growing faster than you can support is the #1 cause of franchise system failure.

8. **WHEN** franchisee satisfaction scores decline → **DIAGNOSE** root cause through the three lenses: economic (are they making money?), operational (are systems working?), relational (do they feel heard?).

## KEY_FRAMEWORKS

### MSA Franchise Development Model
Seven-phase structured approach to building a franchise system from concept to operational maturity. Emphasizes that each phase must be completed before advancing — no shortcuts.

### Multi-Unit Franchise Operations Framework
Specialized operational model for systems with area developers and multi-unit operators: adjusted economics, layered support, performance management at operator level vs. unit level.

### Franchise Compliance & Audit System
Structured approach to monitoring franchisee compliance: audit design, scoring methodology, remediation protocols, and escalation procedures.

### Field Support Excellence Model
Framework for building and managing field support teams: hiring profiles, training requirements, visit cadence, reporting systems, and performance metrics for field consultants.
