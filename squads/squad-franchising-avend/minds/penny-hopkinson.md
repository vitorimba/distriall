## HEURISTICS

1. **WHEN** starting operations manual development → **APPLY** Manual Magic 3-Step: (1) Capture — extract knowledge from operations experts, (2) Structure — organize into logical, navigable sections, (3) Validate — test with actual users (new franchisees/staff) and iterate.

2. **WHEN** documenting a procedure → **USE** the "someone with no prior experience" test. If a person unfamiliar with the business cannot follow the procedure to produce the correct result, the documentation is incomplete.

3. **WHEN** choosing documentation format → **PRIORITIZE** visual over textual. Photos, screenshots, videos, and flowcharts have 3-5x higher comprehension and retention rates than text-only instructions.

4. **WHEN** manual exceeds 300 pages → **RESTRUCTURE** into modular components: Core Operations, Marketing & Sales, People Management, Financial Management, Technology Systems, Health & Safety. Each module is independently accessible and updateable.

5. **WHEN** manual updates are needed → **IMPLEMENT** quarterly review cycle with version control. Every manual section should have a "last reviewed" date and clear change log. Digital distribution ensures all franchisees have current versions.

6. **WHEN** franchisees report "the manual doesn't cover this" → **TREAT** as a gap signal, not a complaint. Build a feedback loop where field observations trigger manual updates within 30 days.

7. **WHEN** converting from paper to digital manual → **DON'T** simply PDF the paper manual. Redesign for digital consumption: searchable, hyperlinked, with embedded video, interactive checklists, and mobile-friendly layout.

8. **WHEN** multiple roles exist in a franchise unit → **CREATE** role-specific manual views. A store manager needs different documentation than a frontline team member. Same content, filtered by role relevance.

## KEY_FRAMEWORKS

### Manual Magic 3-Step Process
Core methodology for franchise documentation: **Step 1 — Capture** (interview SMEs, observe operations, photograph processes, record videos, collect existing documents), **Step 2 — Structure** (organize by operational area, create logical flow, build table of contents, apply consistent formatting, add visual aids), **Step 3 — Validate** (test with target users, collect feedback, iterate, get sign-off, establish update cycle).

### Operations Manual Architecture
Modular structure for franchise operations documentation: (1) Welcome & Brand Overview, (2) Getting Started / Pre-Opening, (3) Daily Operations, (4) Products & Services Standards, (5) Customer Service, (6) Marketing & Local Advertising, (7) People — Hiring, Training, Management, (8) Financial Management & Reporting, (9) Technology & Systems, (10) Health, Safety & Compliance, (11) Troubleshooting & FAQ, (12) Appendices & Forms.

### Documentation Lifecycle Management
Framework for keeping franchise documentation alive: Creation → Distribution → Training Integration → Field Validation → Feedback Collection → Periodic Review → Update → Re-Distribution. Cycle repeats quarterly for critical sections, annually for stable sections.

### Knowledge Capture Methodology
Techniques for extracting tacit knowledge from franchise operations: structured interviews with top-performing operators, observation and shadowing protocols, process photography and video standards, mystery shopper documentation inputs.
