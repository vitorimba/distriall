## HEURISTICS

1. **WHEN** structuring a new franchise system → **DETERMINE** first whether the model is truly franchising vs. licensing vs. distributorship. Misclassification creates FTC compliance exposure and potential franchisee lawsuits.

2. **WHEN** drafting the FDD → **ENSURE** all 23 Items are complete, accurate, and reviewed by franchise-experienced counsel. Pay special attention to Items 3 (litigation), 7 (estimated initial investment), and 19 (financial performance representations).

3. **WHEN** a franchisor wants to make earnings claims → **MANDATE** Item 19 compliance. Any financial performance representation outside the FDD — verbal, in marketing materials, or implied — creates legal liability.

4. **WHEN** trademark strategy is being developed → **SECURE** federal registration (USPTO for US, INPI for Brazil) before any franchise sales. File in all relevant classes. Monitor for infringement proactively.

5. **WHEN** international franchise expansion is planned → **ASSESS** jurisdiction-specific requirements: local franchise laws (not all countries have them), IP registration in-country, local legal counsel, master franchise vs. direct franchising structure.

6. **WHEN** franchise agreement renewal or termination arises → **APPLY** both federal (FTC) and state-specific franchise relationship laws. Some states provide franchisees with renewal rights, cure periods, and good-cause termination protections.

7. **WHEN** technology platforms are part of the franchise system → **ADDRESS** data ownership, privacy compliance (GDPR, LGPD), and technology licensing within the franchise agreement. Data is an increasingly contested franchise asset.

8. **WHEN** franchise disputes arise → **RECOMMEND** graduated dispute resolution: internal mediation → external mediation → arbitration → litigation. Most franchise agreements mandate arbitration — ensure clause is properly drafted.

## KEY_FRAMEWORKS

### Franchise vs. License Decision Framework
Analytical model for determining the appropriate legal structure: franchising (brand control + operational system + ongoing fees = FTC regulated) vs. licensing (IP rights transfer + limited control = generally not FTC regulated) vs. distributorship (product resale + territory = varies by state).

### FDD Compliance Architecture
Comprehensive framework for FDD preparation, annual updates, and state registration management. Covers all 23 Items with emphasis on high-risk disclosures and earnings claims compliance.

### International Franchise Legal Framework
Multi-jurisdictional compliance model: regulatory landscape assessment → IP protection strategy → structure selection (master franchise, area development, direct) → local legal adaptation → ongoing compliance monitoring.

### Franchise IP Protection Strategy
Layered intellectual property protection: trademarks (brand), trade dress (look and feel), trade secrets (operations manual, recipes), copyrights (marketing materials), patents (if applicable). Each layer requires distinct legal instruments and monitoring protocols.
