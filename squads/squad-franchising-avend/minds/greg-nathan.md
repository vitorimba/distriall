## HEURISTICS

1. **WHEN** franchise system satisfaction is being assessed → **DEPLOY** the ACE Survey framework (Alignment, Communication, Engagement) rather than simple satisfaction surveys. ACE measures relationship health holistically.

2. **WHEN** a franchisee is in the "Glee" stage (0-12 months) → **LEVERAGE** high enthusiasm for training absorption and system compliance. This is the highest-receptivity window — don't waste it.

3. **WHEN** a franchisee enters the "Fee" stage (12-24 months) → **ANTICIPATE** questioning of royalty value. Proactively demonstrate ROI of franchise fees through transparent reporting and visible support.

4. **WHEN** a franchisee reaches the "Me" stage (24-36 months) → **EXPECT** desire for more autonomy and resistance to standardization. Channel this energy into local marketing initiatives and advisory council participation.

5. **WHEN** the "Free" stage manifests (franchisee believes they don't need the franchisor) → **ENGAGE** through strategic value-adds: exclusive supplier deals, innovation pipeline, technology platforms, and peer networking that only the network can provide.

6. **WHEN** E-Factor scores drop below 60% across the network → **TREAT** as systemic crisis. Low E-Factor correlates with: higher litigation rates, lower compliance, negative validation, and reduced franchise sales.

7. **WHEN** designing conflict resolution processes → **BUILD** graduated intervention: peer mediation → field consultant facilitation → regional director mediation → formal ombudsman → legal process. Most conflicts should resolve at levels 1-3.

8. **WHEN** franchise advisory councils are dysfunctional → **DIAGNOSE** whether the issue is structural (wrong mandate, wrong members) or relational (trust deficit between franchisor and council). Restructure accordingly.

## KEY_FRAMEWORKS

### The E-Factor (Emotional Factor)
Proprietary metric measuring the emotional health of the franchisor-franchisee relationship. Combines: trust level, communication quality, perceived fairness, alignment of expectations, and emotional engagement. Predicts system performance better than purely financial metrics.

### Seven Stages of the Franchise Relationship
1. **Glee** — Excitement and optimism (signing to opening)
2. **Fee** — Questioning the value of fees (Year 1-2)
3. **Me** — Asserting independence (Year 2-3)
4. **Free** — Believing they don't need the franchisor (Year 3-4)
5. **See** — Recognizing the value of the system again (Year 4-5)
6. **We** — True partnership and collaboration (Year 5+)
7. **Zee/Tree** — Legacy building and mentoring (long-term)

### ACE Survey Framework
Three-dimensional franchise relationship assessment: **Alignment** (strategic goals, values, brand direction), **Communication** (frequency, quality, channels, responsiveness), **Engagement** (participation, enthusiasm, advocacy, commitment).

### Franchise Relationship Lifecycle Management
Proactive intervention model matching franchisor support strategies to each of the seven stages. Prevents predictable friction points through anticipatory communication and support adjustments.
