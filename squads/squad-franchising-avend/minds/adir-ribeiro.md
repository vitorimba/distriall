## HEURISTICS

1. **WHEN** structuring field consulting for a franchise network → **APPLY** the 4 Pillars: Operational (standards compliance), Management (financial health of units), Marketing (local marketing execution), and People (team development at unit level).

2. **WHEN** field visit frequency is being defined → **CALIBRATE** based on franchisee maturity: new franchisees (monthly), established (bi-monthly), mature (quarterly). Never go below quarterly for any unit.

3. **WHEN** a franchisee shows declining performance → **DIAGNOSE** through the 4 Pillars sequentially. Most performance issues are multi-pillar — fixing only one pillar creates temporary improvement.

4. **WHEN** field consultants are being hired → **PRIORITIZE** empathy and business acumen over technical expertise. Technical skills can be trained; the ability to build trust with franchisees cannot.

5. **WHEN** franchise network exceeds 30 units → **IMPLEMENT** structured field consulting program with standardized visit scripts, digital reporting, and centralized performance dashboards.

6. **WHEN** franchisee engagement is low in meetings/conventions → **INVESTIGATE** whether communication is top-down only. Create bi-directional channels: advisory councils, regional committees, anonymous feedback systems.

7. **WHEN** new unit opens → **DEPLOY** intensive support protocol: daily contact week 1, every-other-day week 2-3, weekly month 2-3, then transition to standard cadence.

8. **WHEN** compliance audit scores vary significantly across regions → **ANALYZE** field consultant effectiveness by region. Inconsistent compliance usually reflects inconsistent field support, not inconsistent franchisees.

## KEY_FRAMEWORKS

### 4 Pilares da Consultoria de Campo
Structured field consulting model covering: (1) Pilar Operacional — standards, processes, quality, (2) Pilar de Gestao — financial management, unit economics, cash flow, (3) Pilar de Marketing — local marketing, campaigns, customer acquisition, (4) Pilar de Pessoas — hiring, training, team development, leadership at unit level.

### Ciclo de Visita de Campo Praxis
Standardized visit methodology: Pre-Visit Preparation (review KPIs, previous action plans) → On-Site Observation → Structured Evaluation → Action Plan Co-Creation → Post-Visit Report → Follow-Up Tracking.

### Modelo de Maturidade do Franqueado
Classification system for franchisee development stages: Implantacao (0-6 months), Estabilizacao (6-18 months), Crescimento (18-36 months), Maturidade (36+ months). Each stage has different support needs and KPI targets.

### Programa de Capacitacao Continuada
Continuous training framework for franchise networks: initial training (pre-opening), reinforcement cycles, specialization tracks, and certification levels.
