## HEURISTICS

1. **WHEN** a franchise system has fewer than 20 units → **FOCUS** on founder-led sales. The founder's passion and story are the most powerful sales tools at this stage. Brokers and automated funnels come later.

2. **WHEN** franchise sales stall between 20-50 units → **DIAGNOSE** for Sales Tipping Point barriers: validation issues (existing franchisees not recommending), economic model weaknesses, or support infrastructure gaps.

3. **WHEN** designing the franchise sales process → **BUILD** a structured pipeline: Lead Capture → Initial Qualification → Detailed Application → Discovery Day → Validation → FDD Review → Award. Each stage has clear criteria and timelines.

4. **WHEN** Discovery Day conversion is below 50% → **AUDIT** the experience. Discovery Day should confirm the candidate's emotional and rational decision — if half are walking away, the experience isn't aligned with expectations set during the sales process.

5. **WHEN** franchise broker networks are considered → **EVALUATE** cost-benefit carefully. Broker commissions ($15K-$25K per deal) are justified only when internal sales capacity is maxed and brand validation is strong.

6. **WHEN** franchise candidate asks "what do current franchisees say?" → **CELEBRATE** this question. Strong validation from existing franchisees is the single most powerful sales accelerator. Weak validation is a sales killer.

7. **WHEN** franchise lifecycle reaches maturity (100+ units) → **SHIFT** sales strategy from growth-at-all-costs to strategic growth: market selection, multi-unit operator development, and underperforming territory optimization.

## KEY_FRAMEWORKS

### Franchise Lifecycle Model
Five stages of franchise system development: Inception (0-10 units), Growth (10-50), Tipping Point (50-100), Maturity (100-500), Dominance (500+). Each stage has distinct challenges, required capabilities, and success metrics.

### Sales Tipping Point Theory
The critical inflection point (typically 40-75 units) where franchise systems either achieve self-sustaining growth momentum or stall. Driven by: validation strength, unit economics proof, brand recognition threshold, and operational maturity.

### Street Smart Franchise Sales Process
Practical sales methodology: Lead Nurturing (education-based), Qualification (mutual fit assessment), Discovery Day (experiential validation), Decision Support (validation guidance), Commitment (structured close). Total cycle: 45-90 days.

### Franchise Sales Economics Model
Framework for calculating true cost of franchise sales: marketing spend per lead, lead-to-application rate, application-to-Discovery Day rate, Discovery Day-to-award rate, total cost per awarded franchise.
