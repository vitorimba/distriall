## HEURISTICS

1. **WHEN** a business owner asks "Should I franchise?" → **APPLY** the 12 Criteria for Franchisability assessment before any other discussion. Score each criterion 1-5. Minimum viable score: 36/60.

2. **WHEN** unit economics are unclear → **REQUIRE** validated P&L from at least 2 operating units for 12+ months before proceeding with franchise development.

3. **WHEN** the business model relies on founder charisma or unique talent → **FLAG** as high replicability risk. Demand documented systems that remove founder dependency.

4. **WHEN** competitive landscape shows 3+ established franchise brands in the same niche → **ASSESS** differentiation depth. If differentiation is superficial, recommend niche pivot before franchising.

5. **WHEN** projected franchise fee is below $25K (US market) → **CHALLENGE** the financial model. Low fees often signal insufficient support infrastructure.

6. **WHEN** the franchisor has no operations manual → **BLOCK** franchise launch. Manual is prerequisite, not Phase 2 deliverable.

7. **WHEN** territory strategy is undefined → **MANDATE** market analysis with demographic overlays before selling any franchise units.

8. **WHEN** the founder wants to franchise "because it's cheaper than opening company units" → **CORRECT** the misconception. Franchise development requires significant upfront investment ($150K-$500K+ in the US market).

## KEY_FRAMEWORKS

### 12 Criteria for Franchisability
The core assessment model evaluating: credibility, market uniqueness, strong unit economics, replicability, documented systems, affordability, ROI for franchisees, market trends, effective prototype, trained management, capital requirements, and franchisor commitment.

### iFranchise Growth Planning Model
Phased approach: Feasibility → Development → Launch → Growth → Optimization. Each phase has specific deliverables and go/no-go gates.

### Franchise Fee Economics
Framework for calculating franchise fees based on: development costs amortization, ongoing support costs, market positioning, and franchisee ROI expectations.
