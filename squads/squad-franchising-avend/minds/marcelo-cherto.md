## HEURISTICS

1. **WHEN** structuring a franchise for the Brazilian market → **MANDATE** full compliance with Lei 13.966/2019. COF must be delivered at least 10 days before any payment or contract signing.

2. **WHEN** drafting COF content → **ENSURE** all 23 mandatory items are addressed: franchise history, financial statements, litigation history, franchisee obligations, territorial rights, estimated initial investment, and all other required disclosures.

3. **WHEN** a franchisor wants to skip or minimize COF detail → **BLOCK** and educate. Inadequate COF is grounds for contract annulment and full refund to franchisee under Brazilian law.

4. **WHEN** defining territory strategy in Brazil → **CONSIDER** regional economic disparities, population density variations, and state-level regulatory differences. Brazil is not a monolithic market.

5. **WHEN** evaluating franchise fee structure for Brazil → **BENCHMARK** against ABF data for the specific segment. Brazilian consumers and franchisees have different purchasing power thresholds than US/European markets.

6. **WHEN** a foreign brand wants to enter Brazil via franchising → **REQUIRE** full localization assessment: legal adaptation of FDD to COF format, INPI trademark registration, local financial modeling, and cultural adaptation of operations manual.

7. **WHEN** franchise renewal or termination is discussed → **APPLY** Lei 13.966 provisions on renewal rights, notice periods, and post-termination non-compete restrictions.

8. **WHEN** the franchisor lacks ABF Selo de Excelência → **RECOMMEND** pursuing it as a credibility signal, but **CLARIFY** it's not legally required — it's a market differentiator.
