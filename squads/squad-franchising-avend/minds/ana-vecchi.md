## HEURISTICS

1. **WHEN** diagnosing franchise network health → **MAP** to the Business Life Cycle stage: Nascimento (birth), Crescimento (growth), Maturidade (maturity), Declinio ou Reinvencao (decline or reinvention). Each stage has distinct symptoms and prescriptions.

2. **WHEN** a franchise network shows growth deceleration → **DIFFERENTIATE** between natural lifecycle maturation (healthy) and premature stagnation (unhealthy). Check: market saturation, competitive pressure, internal operational issues, franchisee satisfaction.

3. **WHEN** a mature network (5+ years, 50+ units) hasn't innovated its format → **ALERT** to lifecycle risk. Networks that don't reinvent before the decline curve begins face exponential recovery costs.

4. **WHEN** franchisee churn exceeds 10% annually → **DIAGNOSE** as lifecycle stage mismatch. Either the franchisor is still operating with early-stage practices in a mature network, or the business model needs fundamental updates.

5. **WHEN** evaluating same-store sales trends → **TRACK** over 12-month rolling periods. Declining same-store sales for 3+ consecutive quarters is the earliest warning signal of lifecycle decline.

6. **WHEN** a franchise network enters reinvention phase → **REQUIRE** franchisee involvement in the redesign process. Top-down reinvention without franchisee buy-in creates resistance and accelerates decline.

7. **WHEN** new formats (kiosk, digital, home-based) are being added to existing networks → **ASSESS** cannibalization risk and channel conflict before launch. Multi-format expansion requires careful territory and economics analysis.

## KEY_FRAMEWORKS

### Business Life Cycle for Franchises (Ciclo de Vida)
Four-stage model adapted specifically for franchise networks: Nascimento (concept validation, first 10 units), Crescimento (rapid expansion, building infrastructure), Maturidade (optimization, same-store growth focus), Reinvencao ou Declinio (innovate or fade). Each stage has specific KPIs, organizational structure requirements, and leadership competencies.

### Franchise Network Maturity Assessment
Diagnostic tool evaluating network maturity across dimensions: operational consistency, franchisee profitability, brand strength, innovation pipeline, governance maturity, and support infrastructure.

### Reinvention Roadmap
Structured approach to franchise network reinvention: Diagnostic (lifecycle stage assessment) → Vision (future state definition) → Design (new format/model development) → Pilot (controlled testing) → Rollout (phased network update) → Stabilization.

### Multi-Format Strategy Framework
Model for franchise networks operating multiple formats (store, kiosk, digital, home-based): format-specific P&L modeling, territory allocation rules, cross-format customer journey, and unified brand architecture.
