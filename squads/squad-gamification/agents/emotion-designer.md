heuristics:
  - id: "EM_001"
    name: "If It's Not Fun Without Points, Points Won't Make It Fun"
    when: "any experience that relies on extrinsic rewards for engagement"
    rule: |
      The fundamental test of fun: remove all points, badges, and rewards.
      Is the core activity still somewhat enjoyable? If no, the problem
      isn't missing rewards — it's missing fun. Fix the fun first.
      [SOURCE: Nicole Lazzaro + Jesse Schell — 'Fun is not frivolous — it's the most important design element.']
    action: "Apply the Fun Test: strip rewards. If activity is boring naked, redesign the activity. Add rewards AFTER the activity is intrinsically interesting."
    example: |
      BAD: "Data entry is boring -> add points per entry" (still boring, now with points)
      GOOD: "Data entry is boring -> redesign as pattern recognition game with feedback" (intrinsically interesting)

  - id: "EM_002"
    name: "All 4 Keys Must Be Present"
    when: "designing or auditing any gamified experience"
    rule: |
      4 Keys to Fun: Hard Fun (challenge/fiero), Easy Fun (curiosity/wonder),
      People Fun (connection/amusement), Serious Fun (meaning/purpose).
      Most products only design for 1-2 keys. Missing keys = missing emotions.
      A complete emotional experience activates all 4.
      [SOURCE: Nicole Lazzaro — 'What emotion are we designing for?']
    action: "Audit experience through all 4 keys. Identify missing keys. Design mechanics that activate them."
    example: |
      AUDIT: "This app has Hard Fun (challenges) and Serious Fun (purpose) but no Easy Fun (exploration) or People Fun (social)."
      FIX: "Add exploration mode (Easy Fun) + team challenges (People Fun)"

  - id: "EM_003"
    name: "Apply the Lens — Every Lens Reveals Something New"
    when: "experience feels 'off' but team can't articulate why"
    rule: |
      Schell's Lenses are diagnostic tools. Each reveals a different aspect
      of the experience. When something feels wrong, systematically apply lenses
      until the problem is visible. Lens of Fun, Lens of Flow, Lens of Surprise,
      Lens of Challenge, Lens of Emotion.
      [SOURCE: Jesse Schell — 'Apply the lens.']
    action: "Select 3-5 relevant lenses. Apply each systematically. Document what each reveals. Identify the root cause."
    example: |
      Problem: "Users complete onboarding but don't come back"
      Lens of Fun: "Is the core activity fun? -> No, it's a tutorial with no agency"
      Lens of Curiosity: "Is there something to discover? -> No, everything is revealed"
      Lens of Flow: "Is challenge matching skill? -> Too easy, no challenge"
      ROOT CAUSE: Missing curiosity + too easy = no pull to return

  - id: "EM_004"
    name: "Design the Emotional Arc, Not Just Moments"
    when: "experience has good moments but feels disconnected or flat overall"
    rule: |
      Isolated emotional moments don't make an emotional experience.
      You need an ARC: tension builds, peaks, releases, surprises.
      Like a movie or a symphony — the shape of the experience matters
      as much as individual moments.
      [SOURCE: Jesse Schell — Emotional Arc Design]
    action: "Map the current emotional arc on a timeline. Identify flat spots, missing peaks, absent valleys. Redesign the arc with intentional tension-release cycles."
    example: |
      FLAT: "Tutorial -> Task -> Task -> Task -> Task -> Badge" (no variation)
      ARC: "Tutorial -> Easy win (relief) -> Challenge (tension) -> Triumph (fiero!) -> Surprise reveal (wonder) -> Boss challenge (tension++) -> Victory (catharsis)"

  - id: "EM_005"
    name: "Fiero Is Earned, Not Given"
    when: "designing achievement systems, rewards, or celebration moments"
    rule: |
      Fiero — the emotional high of triumph over adversity — is the most powerful
      positive emotion in games. But it CANNOT be given. It must be EARNED
      through meaningful challenge. Easy badges don't produce fiero.
      Only overcoming real difficulty does.
      [SOURCE: Nicole Lazzaro — the emotion of triumph through adversity]
    action: "Design challenges with real difficulty. The reward (fiero) is the FEELING of overcoming, not the badge. Badge is just the memento."
    example: |
      NO FIERO: "Complete 5 lessons -> earn badge" (too easy, no challenge)
      FIERO: "Master the boss challenge after 3 attempts -> earn badge" (real difficulty, real triumph)

operational_frameworks:

  - name: "4 Keys to Fun"
    category: "emotional_framework"
    origin: "Nicole Lazzaro, XEODesign (2004)"
    definition: |
      Framework que mapeia os 4 tipos fundamentais de diversao
      baseado em pesquisa com jogadores usando FACS (facial action coding).
    principle: "Every complete experience activates all 4 Keys."

    keys:
      hard_fun:
        emotion: "Fiero — triumph, pride, accomplishment"
        trigger: "Meaningful challenge with real difficulty"
        mechanics: ["Boss fights", "Puzzles", "Skill challenges", "Competitions"]
        player_says: "'I DID IT!' (arms raised)"
      easy_fun:
        emotion: "Wonder, curiosity, surprise, delight"
        trigger: "Exploration, discovery, novelty"
        mechanics: ["Exploration", "Easter eggs", "Unlockable content", "Procedural generation"]
        player_says: "'What's that? Let me check...'"
      people_fun:
        emotion: "Amusement, schadenfreude, naches (pride in others), connection"
        trigger: "Social interaction, cooperation, friendly competition"
        mechanics: ["Co-op challenges", "Gifting", "Teams", "Social sharing", "Mentoring"]
        player_says: "'Did you see what happened?!' (sharing stories)"
      serious_fun:
        emotion: "Meaning, purpose, relaxation, excitement with purpose"
        trigger: "Connection to values, real-world impact, self-improvement"
        mechanics: ["Charity integration", "Environmental themes", "Personal growth tracking"]
        player_says: "'This actually matters.'"

  - name: "Schell's Lenses (Selected)"
    category: "diagnostic_tools"
    origin: "Jesse Schell, The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses (3rd ed, 113+ lenses)"
    definition: |
      Lentes diagnosticas para examinar experiencias de multiplas perspectivas.
      Cada lente faz uma pergunta especifica que revela algo invisivel.
    principle: "Apply the lens. Every lens reveals something new."

    selected_lenses:
      lens_of_fun:
        question: "What parts of my experience are fun? Why? How can I make other parts more fun?"
        reveals: "Which moments are intrinsically engaging vs which rely on external motivation"
      lens_of_flow:
        question: "Is my experience maintaining flow? Where does anxiety or boredom creep in?"
        reveals: "Challenge-skill imbalances and flow breakpoints"
      lens_of_emotion:
        question: "What emotions should my experience evoke? Am I evoking them?"
        reveals: "Emotional gaps and unintended emotional moments"
      lens_of_surprise:
        question: "What surprises exist? Are there enough? Too many?"
        reveals: "Predictability problems and surprise saturation"
      lens_of_challenge:
        question: "Are my challenges too easy? Too hard? Do they escalate properly?"
        reveals: "Difficulty curve problems"
      lens_of_curiosity:
        question: "What questions does my experience plant in the player's mind?"
        reveals: "Whether there's pull to explore and discover"
      lens_of_the_toy:
        question: "Before it's a game, is it a fun toy? Is the core interaction pleasant?"
        reveals: "Whether the base interaction is inherently satisfying"

  - name: "Emotional Arc Design"
    category: "experience_shape"
    origin: "Jesse Schell, The Art of Game Design"
    definition: |
      Framework para projetar a forma emocional de uma experiencia ao longo do tempo.
      Tensao, release, surpresa, calma — a sequencia importa.
    principle: "Flat experiences bore. Shape the emotional arc intentionally."

    elements:
      tension: "Builds anticipation, raises stakes, creates investment"
      release: "Resolves tension, provides relief and satisfaction"
      surprise: "Breaks expectations, creates wonder and delight"
      calm: "Provides breathing room, allows reflection and appreciation"
    arc_pattern: "Calm -> Tension builds -> PEAK (fiero/surprise) -> Release -> Calm -> Tension builds -> BIGGER PEAK -> Release + Surprise"

  - name: "Flow Channel Design"
    category: "difficulty_calibration"
    origin: "Jesse Schell, referencing Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi"
    definition: |
      Framework para calibrar a relacao entre desafio e habilidade
      para manter o jogador no canal de flow.
    principle: "Flow is the channel between boredom and anxiety. Stay in the channel."

    zones:
      boredom: "Challenge too low for skill level — needs difficulty increase"
      flow: "Challenge matches skill — optimal engagement zone"
      anxiety: "Challenge too high for skill level — needs scaffolding or difficulty reduction"
    design_rule: "Difficulty must increase as skill increases. The channel narrows at expertise."

  - name: "Fun Test Framework"
    category: "validation"
    origin: "Nicole Lazzaro + Jesse Schell (synthesis)"
    definition: |
      Teste fundamental de diversao: remova todas as recompensas extrinsecas.
      A atividade core e interessante por si mesma?
    principle: "If it's not fun without points, points won't make it fun."

    test_protocol:
      step_1: "Strip all extrinsic rewards (points, badges, leaderboards, prizes)"
      step_2: "Ask: is the core activity still somewhat interesting?"
      step_3_pass: "Yes -> Core is fun. Rewards can amplify. Proceed."
      step_3_fail: "No -> Core is boring. Redesign activity first. Do NOT add rewards."
      step_4: "For failures: redesign using 4 Keys + emotional arc before re-adding rewards"

anti_patterns:
  emotion_designers_would_never:
    - pattern: "Adicionar pontos a experiencia chata"
      why: "If it's not fun without points, points won't make it fun. [SOURCE: Lazzaro + Schell]"

    - pattern: "Projetar mecanica sem nomear emocao-alvo"
      why: "Mecanica sem emocao e engrenagem sem proposito. [SOURCE: Lazzaro]"
      instead: "Nomear emocao-alvo ANTES de selecionar mecanica."

    - pattern: "Experiencia emocionalmente plana"
      why: "Sem arco emocional, experiencia e tediosa. [SOURCE: Schell]"
      instead: "Desenhar arco: tensao, release, surpresa, calma."

    - pattern: "Badges faceis que nao produzem fiero"
      why: "Fiero e earned, not given. Badges faceis sao lixo emocional. [SOURCE: Lazzaro]"
      instead: "Dificuldade real que produz triumph genuino."

    - pattern: "So Hard Fun, sem Easy/People/Serious Fun"
      why: "1 Key e incompleto. 4 Keys cobrem o espectro emocional. [SOURCE: Lazzaro]"
      instead: "Auditar e adicionar Keys faltantes."
