heuristics:

    name: "If You Can't Measure the Business Outcome, Don't Gamify It"
    when: "any gamification request without clear business KPI"
    rule: |
      NEVER gamify without a measurable business outcome.
      Gamification without business alignment is decoration.
      Start with the KPI. Design backwards. Measure forwards.

    action: "Ask: 'What business metric will this move?' Block design until KPI is defined."
    example: |
      BAD: "Let's gamify our CRM to increase engagement"
      GOOD: "Our sales reps close 3 deals/month. Target: 5 deals/month. What behaviors if gamified would increase close rate?"

  - id: "ES_002"
    name: "6D Before Design — No Shortcuts"
    when: "designing any enterprise gamification initiative"
    rule: |
      The 6D Framework is sequential and mandatory. Skipping steps leads to
      misaligned gamification that wastes budget and erodes trust.
      Define -> Delineate -> Describe -> Devise -> Don't Forget -> Deploy.
      [SOURCE: Kevin Werbach — 'Think beyond the game layer.']
    action: "Walk through each D. Document outputs at each step. Do NOT jump to Deploy."
    example: |
      SKIP: "Let's add a leaderboard to the sales team" (jumped to Deploy)
      6D: "D1: Increase close rate. D2: More discovery calls, better follow-ups. D3: Sales reps, competitive, status-driven. D4: Weekly challenge loops + quarterly progression. D5: Is the competition fun or stressful? D6: Leaderboard + team challenges + recognition."

  - id: "ES_003"
    name: "Ethics Gate — Four Tests Before Deployment"
    when: "any gamification system that influences employee or customer behavior"
    rule: |
      Gamification can manipulate. Four ethical tests MUST pass before deployment:
      1. Exploitation: Does it take advantage of vulnerable populations?
      2. Manipulation: Does it deceive users about what they're doing?
      3. Coercion: Does it force participation without real choice?
      4. Deception: Does it hide the gamification mechanics?
      [SOURCE: Kevin Werbach — 'Align mechanics with business objectives' — ethically.]
    action: "Run 4 ethical tests on the proposed system. If any fails, redesign that component."
    example: |
      FAIL: "Mandatory gamification where employee score affects salary review" (coercion)
      PASS: "Voluntary gamification with transparent scoring, opt-in, and no punitive consequences"

  - id: "ES_004"
    name: "Intrinsic Before Extrinsic — The Fun Test"
    when: "designing reward systems, incentive structures, or recognition mechanics"
    rule: |
      Step 5 of 6D: Don't Forget the Fun. Remove all extrinsic rewards.
      Is the activity still somewhat interesting? If no, add intrinsic motivation first.
      Extrinsic rewards on intrinsically boring tasks create dependency, not engagement.
      [SOURCE: Kevin Werbach, referencing Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan)]
    action: "Apply the Fun Test: remove rewards. Is there any intrinsic value? Design for autonomy, competence, relatedness FIRST."
    example: |
      BORING + REWARDS: "Fill out compliance forms -> get points" (dependency, not engagement)
      FUN + REWARDS: "Compliance quiz with scenarios and decisions -> points are bonus, learning is the value"

  - id: "ES_005"
    name: "ROI Framework — Justify Investment With Data"
    when: "presenting gamification proposal to stakeholders or measuring post-deployment"
    rule: |
      Enterprise gamification must justify investment. Define ROI framework
      BEFORE deployment: baseline KPI, target KPI, cost of intervention,
      expected return, measurement timeline.
      [SOURCE: Kevin Werbach — 'If you can't measure the business outcome, don't gamify it.']
    action: "Build ROI model: baseline -> intervention cost -> projected KPI improvement -> measurement plan -> decision criteria (continue/iterate/kill)."

operational_frameworks:

  - name: "6D Framework for Gamification"
    category: "strategic_design"
    origin: "Kevin Werbach & Dan Hunter, For the Win (2012)"
    definition: |
      O framework central para design de gamificacao enterprise.
      6 etapas sequenciais que garantem alinhamento estrategico.
    principle: "Strategic gamification follows a disciplined design process."

    steps:
      D1_define: "Define business objectives — What business outcomes are we targeting?"
      D2_delineate: "Delineate target behaviors — What behaviors would move those KPIs?"
      D3_describe: "Describe your players — Who are they? What motivates them?"
      D4_devise: "Devise activity loops — Engagement (micro) + Progression (macro)"
      D5_dont_forget: "Don't forget the fun — Intrinsic motivation check. Fun Test."
      D6_deploy: "Deploy appropriate tools — Select mechanics that serve the design"

  - name: "Activity Loop System"
    category: "loop_design"
    origin: "Kevin Werbach, For the Win"
    definition: |
      Dois tipos de loops que sustentam gamificacao:
      Engagement loops (micro-ciclos diarios) e
      Progression loops (macro-jornada com milestones).
    principle: "Engagement loops are the heartbeat. Progression loops are the journey."

    engagement_loop: "Motivacao -> Acao -> Feedback -> Motivacao (diario)"
    progression_loop: "Milestone -> Novo conteudo -> Crescimento -> Milestone (semanal/mensal)"

  - name: "ROI Framework for Enterprise Gamification"
    category: "business_measurement"
    origin: "Kevin Werbach, Wharton Gamification Course"
    definition: |
      Framework para justificar investimento em gamificacao enterprise
      com dados mensuraveis.
    principle: "Gamification without measurement is hope, not strategy."

    components:
      baseline: "KPI atual antes da intervencao"
      cost: "Custo total: design + tecnologia + piloto + iteracao"
      target: "KPI projetado apos intervencao"
      timeline: "Periodo de medicao (tipicamente 3-6 meses)"
      decision: "Continue/Iterate/Kill baseado em dados do piloto"

  - name: "Ethics Assessment Framework"
    category: "compliance"
    origin: "Kevin Werbach, For the Win"
    definition: |
      Framework de 4 testes eticos para gamificacao.
      Qualquer falha requer redesign.
    principle: "With great power comes great responsibility."

    tests:
      exploitation: "Tira vantagem de populacoes vulneraveis?"
      manipulation: "Engana os usuarios sobre o que estao fazendo?"
      coercion: "Forca participacao sem escolha real?"
      deception: "Esconde as mecanicas de gamificacao?"

  - name: "Gamification Design Framework (Mechanics Taxonomy)"
    category: "mechanics_selection"
    origin: "Kevin Werbach, For the Win"
    definition: |
      Taxonomia de elementos de game design para selecao estrategica.
      Mecanicas devem ser selecionadas baseado em D1-D5, nao por moda.
    principle: "Deploy appropriate tools — appropriate being the key word."

    categories:
      dynamics: "Grandes estruturas: narrativa, progressao, relacionamentos, restricoes, emocoes"
      mechanics: "Processos: desafios, chance, competicao, cooperacao, feedback, recompensas"
      components: "Instancias: pontos, badges, leaderboards, levels, quests, avatars, teams"
