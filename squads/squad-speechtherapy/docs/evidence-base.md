# Evidence Base — Squad SpeechTherapy

> Resumo das evidencias cientificas que fundamentam cada protocolo do squad.
> Todos os agentes sao baseados em pesquisadores reais com publicacoes peer-reviewed.

---

## Protocolos e Evidencia

### SOVT (Semi-Occluded Vocal Tract) — @ingo-titze

**Base cientifica:** Exercicios com oclusao parcial do trato vocal aumentam a impedancia acustica supraglotica, reduzindo o limiar de pressao fonatoria e promovendo vibracao mais eficiente das pregas vocais.

**Estudos-chave:**
- Titze, I. R. (2006). Voice training and therapy with a semi-occluded vocal tract. *JSLHR*, 49(2), 448-459.
- Titze, I. R. (2018). Major benefits of semi-occluded vocal tract exercises. *JASA*, 144(3), EL95-EL100.
- Guzman et al. (2013). Efficacy of water resistance therapy in subjects with occupational voice disorders.

**Nivel de evidencia:** Moderado-Alto (multiplos estudos experimentais + RCTs)

---

### VFE (Vocal Function Exercises) — @joseph-stemple

**Base cientifica:** 4 exercicios sistematicos (warm-up, stretching, contracting, power) que fortalecem e equilibram os subsistemas laringeos (respiratorio, fonatoria, ressonancia).

**Estudos-chave:**
- Stemple, J. C. et al. (1994). Efficacy of vocal function exercises as a method of improving voice production. *JVLD*, 8, 271-278.
- Roy, N. et al. (2001). An evaluation of the effects of two treatment approaches for teachers with voice disorders. *JSLHR*, 44, 286-296.
- Pedrosa, V. et al. (2016). Vocal function exercises effectiveness: A systematic review.

**Nivel de evidencia:** Alto (RCTs confirmam eficacia para nodulos e fadiga vocal)

---

### LMRVT (Lessac-Madsen Resonant Voice Therapy) — @katherine-verdolini

**Base cientifica:** Terapia de voz ressonante foca em producao vocal com vibracao anterior maxima e impacto minimo nas pregas vocais. Protocolo de 8 sessoes estruturadas.

**Estudos-chave:**
- Verdolini-Marston, K. et al. (1995). Preliminary study of two methods of treatment for laryngeal nodules. *JVLD*, 9(1), 74-85.
- Verdolini Abbott, K. et al. (2012). Lessac-Madsen Resonant Voice Therapy: Clinician Manual. Plural Publishing.
- Chen, S. H. et al. (2007). Evaluation of resonant voice therapy for teachers with voice disorders.

**Nivel de evidencia:** Alto (RCT validado, manual clinico publicado)

---

### CTT (Conversation Training Therapy) — @jackie-gartner-schmidt

**Base cientifica:** Abordagem de aprendizagem motora que foca na generalizacao de tecnicas vocais para a fala conversacional real. 6 principios (tenets) guiam a terapia.

**Estudos-chave:**
- Gartner-Schmidt, J. L. et al. (2016). Development and validation of CTT for voice disorders.
- Gillespie, A. & Gartner-Schmidt, J. (2018). Conversation Training Therapy for treatment of dysphonia.

**Nivel de evidencia:** Moderado (estudos de validacao + caso clinico)

---

### Manual Therapy — @jacob-lieberman

**Base cientifica:** Terapia manual laringea (MTD) reduz tensao musculoesqueletica perilaringea que contribui para disfonia por tensao muscular. Tecnicas de palpacao, alongamento e liberacao circumlaringea.

**Estudos-chave:**
- Lieberman, J. (1998). Principles and techniques of manual therapy. *Voice*, 12(2), 161-172.
- Roy, N. et al. (2009). Manual laryngeal musculoskeletal therapy for functional dysphonia.
- Van Lierde, K. et al. (2010). Effect of manual circumlaryngeal therapy.

**Nivel de evidencia:** Moderado (multiplos estudos experimentais)

---

### Avaliacao e Higiene Vocal — @mara-behlau

**Base cientifica:** Avaliacao perceptivo-auditiva (GRBAS/CAPE-V), analise acustica, e protocolos de autoavaliacao (VHI-10) como base para diagnostico e monitoramento.

**Estudos-chave:**
- Behlau, M. et al. (2001). Voz: O Livro do Especialista. Revinter.
- Behlau, M. et al. (2011). Epidemiology of voice disorders in teachers and non-teachers in Brazil.
- Consensus Auditory-Perceptual Evaluation of Voice (CAPE-V) — ASHA SID3.

**Nivel de evidencia:** Alto (protocolos padronizados internacionalmente)

## Protocolo de 12 Semanas (Nodulos Vocais)

| Fase | Semanas | Foco | Agentes | Evidencia |
|------|---------|------|---------|-----------|
| 1 — Estabilizacao | 1-3 | Higiene + SOVT conservador | mara-behlau, ingo-titze | Titze 2006, Behlau 2001 |
| 2 — Fortalecimento | 4-6 | VFE + LMRVT | joseph-stemple, katherine-verdolini | Stemple 1994, Verdolini 2012 |
| 3 — Generalizacao | 7-9 | CTT + progressao | jackie-gartner-schmidt | Gartner-Schmidt 2016 |
| 4 — Manutencao | 10-12 | Autonomia + alta | speechtherapy-chief | Consenso clinico |
