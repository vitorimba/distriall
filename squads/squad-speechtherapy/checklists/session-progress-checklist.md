## 10. Red Flags Check

**This section is MANDATORY. Every session must include a red flags screen.**

### 10.1 Critical Red Flags (immediate action required)

- [ ] **No critical red flags present** (check all that apply — any checked = ALERT)
  - [ ] ALERT: Blood in saliva, sputum, or when coughing
  - [ ] ALERT: Sudden complete voice loss (aphonia)
  - [ ] ALERT: Severe pain during phonation (>= 7/10)
  - [ ] ALERT: Difficulty breathing or stridor
  - [ ] ALERT: Suspected airway compromise
  - [ ] ALERT: Rapid onset swelling (neck/throat)

**If ANY critical red flag is checked:**
1. STOP all exercises immediately
2. Document details
3. Refer to ENT/emergency as appropriate
4. Notify speechtherapy-chief
5. Do NOT resume exercises until cleared by physician

### 10.2 Warning Signs (monitor closely)

- [ ] **Warning signs assessed**
  - [ ] WARNING: Voice quality declining over 2+ consecutive sessions
  - [ ] WARNING: VHI increasing despite treatment
  - [ ] WARNING: Pain increasing over time
  - [ ] WARNING: New onset of diplophonia
  - [ ] WARNING: Persistent vocal fatigue (> 4 hours post-exercise)
  - [ ] WARNING: Patient non-compliance (< 50% for 2+ weeks)
  - [ ] WARNING: New neurological symptoms (tremor, spasms)
  - [ ] WARNING: Significant weight loss or systemic illness

**If ANY warning sign is checked:**
1. Document in session notes
2. Discuss with speechtherapy-chief
3. Consider ENT re-evaluation
4. Adjust treatment plan as needed
5. Increase monitoring frequency

### 10.3 Psychosocial Red Flags

- [ ] **Psychosocial screen**
  - [ ] WARNING: Patient expresses hopelessness about recovery
  - [ ] WARNING: Significant anxiety affecting compliance
  - [ ] WARNING: Depression symptoms affecting daily function
  - [ ] WARNING: Social withdrawal due to voice
  - [ ] WARNING: Occupational crisis (job loss, income impact)

**If psychosocial red flag checked:**
1. Acknowledge patient concerns
2. Document
3. Consider referral to psychologist/counselor
4. Adjust expectations and timeline if needed
