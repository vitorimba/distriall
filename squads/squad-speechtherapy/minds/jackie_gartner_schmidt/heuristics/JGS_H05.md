# Heuristic: Prosody-Last Integration

- **ID:** JGS_H05
- **Name:** Prosody-Last Integration
- **Agent:** jackie-gartner-schmidt
- **Severity:** MEDIUM

## When
Clear speech and auditory awareness are stable but voice sounds robotic or effortful.

## Rule
Shift tenet focus to Prosody — restore natural melody, stress, and rhythm in conversation.

## Rationale
Functional voice must sound natural or patient will avoid using it socially.

## Action
1. Identificar: fala clara mas robotica ou com esforco
2. Mudar foco para tenet de Prosodia
3. Trabalhar melodia, ritmo e enfase naturais na conversa
4. Objetivo: voz que soa humana, nao terapeutica
5. Integrar prosodia em contextos conversacionais reais
