# Heuristic: BTG-Fade Protocol

- **ID:** JGS_H04
- **Name:** BTG-Fade Protocol
- **Agent:** jackie-gartner-schmidt
- **Severity:** HIGH

## When
Patient has achieved >80% correct production with BTG active for 2+ sessions.

## Rule
Begin systematic BTG fading: reduce gesture amplitude, then frequency, then eliminate.

## Rationale
External cues must be faded or patient becomes cue-dependent — not autonomous.

## Action
1. Confirmar: >80% producao correta com BTG por 2+ sessoes
2. Fase 1: reduzir amplitude do gesto
3. Fase 2: reduzir frequencia do gesto
4. Fase 3: eliminar gesto completamente
5. Se reversao: voltar uma fase e estabilizar
6. Objetivo: autonomia do paciente sem cues externos
