# Heuristic: VETO — Script-Only Request

- **ID:** JGS_VETO_03
- **Name:** VETO — Script-Only Request
- **Agent:** jackie-gartner-schmidt
- **Severity:** SOFT_BLOCK

## When
Patient requests script-based or drill repetition exercises exclusively.

## Rule
Explain CTT rationale. If patient refuses conversation-based approach after explanation,
document and consider whether CTT is appropriate match or handoff needed.

## Rationale
CTT requer conversa como estimulo. Drill isolado nao e compativel com a abordagem.

## Action
1. Explicar racional do CTT: conversa e o estimulo, nao drill
2. Citar evidencia: pratica variada supera pratica em bloco (Schmidt & Lee)
3. Se paciente recusa apos explicacao: documentar
4. Considerar se CTT e match adequado ou se handoff e necessario
5. Nao forcar abordagem — respeitar autonomia do paciente
