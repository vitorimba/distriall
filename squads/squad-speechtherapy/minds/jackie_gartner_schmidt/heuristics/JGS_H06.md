# Heuristic: Rapport-Reset Signal

- **ID:** JGS_H06
- **Name:** Rapport-Reset Signal
- **Agent:** jackie-gartner-schmidt
- **Severity:** HIGH

## When
Patient becomes avoidant, monosyllabic, or laughs nervously about voice attempts.

## Rule
Pause technical work. Rebuild rapport tenet: explore patient's experience, normalize struggle.

## Rationale
Psychological safety is the container for motor learning — without it, learning stops.

## Action
1. Identificar sinais: evitamento, respostas monossilabicas, riso nervoso
2. Pausar trabalho tecnico imediatamente
3. Reconstruir rapport: explorar experiencia do paciente
4. Normalizar a luta: "isso e parte do processo"
5. So retomar tecnica quando seguranca psicologica estiver restabelecida
