# Heuristic: Professional-Voice Intensity Calibration

- **ID:** JGS_H07
- **Name:** Professional-Voice Intensity Calibration
- **Agent:** jackie-gartner-schmidt
- **Severity:** HIGH

## When
Patient is a high-demand professional voice user (teacher, lawyer, broadcaster, executive).

## Rule
Assess occupational vocal demands first; calibrate CTT intensity to match performance context.

## Rationale
Recovery targets must match real-world demand — not just clinic-level voice quality.

## Action
1. Avaliar demandas vocais ocupacionais primeiro
2. Mapear contextos de alta demanda (aula, tribunal, broadcast, reunioes)
3. Calibrar intensidade do CTT para corresponder ao contexto real
4. Role-play de situacoes profissionais especificas
5. Sucesso = voz funcional no ambiente de trabalho, nao apenas na clinica
