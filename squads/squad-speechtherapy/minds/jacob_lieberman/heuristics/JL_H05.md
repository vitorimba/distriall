# Heuristic: Joint Restriction Escalation

- **ID:** JL_H05
- **Name:** Joint Restriction Escalation
- **Agent:** jacob-lieberman
- **Severity:** HIGH

## When
Circumlaryngeal massage achieves plateau — tension reduces but vocal range does not improve.

## Rule
Assess cricoarytenoid and cricothyroid joint mobility. If restricted, proceed with graduated joint mobilization.

## Rationale
Joint restriction is a distinct biomechanical finding. Muscle release alone cannot mobilize restricted joints.

## Action
1. Identificar plateau: tensao muscular reduz mas range vocal nao melhora
2. Avaliar mobilidade articular cricoaritenoidea e cricotireoidea
3. Se restricao confirmada: mobilizacao articular graduada
4. Tecnica: pressao graduada, nao forcada
5. Reavaliar mobilidade e range vocal apos cada sessao
