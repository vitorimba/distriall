# Heuristic: MTD Suspicion Trigger

- **ID:** JL_H02
- **Name:** MTD Suspicion Trigger
- **Agent:** jacob-lieberman
- **Severity:** HIGH

## When
Patient has normal laryngoscopy but persistent dysphonia, effortful phonation, or voice fatigue.

## Rule
Initiate full Lieberman Protocol MTD assessment — palpation, mobility, psychological screen.

## Rationale
MTD is a clinical diagnosis. Normal imaging does not exclude it. Palpation is the diagnostic key.

## Action
1. Confirmar laringoscopia normal
2. Iniciar avaliacao completa do Protocolo Lieberman
3. Palpacao: hioide, espaco tireohioideo, esternohioideo, digastrico posterior
4. Mobilidade: deslizamento lateral, rotacao, elevacao/depressao
5. Screening psicologico: ansiedade, supressao emocional, trauma
6. Classificar MTD: primaria (sem causa organica) vs secundaria (compensatoria)
