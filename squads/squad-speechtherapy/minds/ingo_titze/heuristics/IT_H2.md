# Heuristic: Vocal Fatigue — Straw Protocol

- **ID:** IT_H2
- **Name:** Vocal Fatigue — Straw Protocol
- **Agent:** ingo-titze
- **Severity:** HIGH

## When
Patient reports vocal fatigue, end-of-day voice deterioration, or teacher's voice.

## Rule
Prescribe straw phonation in water (Water Resistance Straw Phonation — WRSP).
Depth: 3-5 cm. Duration: 5 minutes, 3x daily.
The water resistance adds back-pressure that maximizes inertive reactance benefit.

## Rationale
Fatigue = vocal dose overload. WRSP restores oscillation efficiency with minimal dose.

## Action
1. Select semiflexible straw, narrow diameter (5-6 mm)
2. Water depth: 3-4 cm (mark with tape)
3. Morning (pre-work): 3 min descending glissandos
4. Midday (break): 2 min sustained note at comfortable pitch
5. Evening (post-load): 5 min free WRSP (glissandos + sustained)
6. Stop signs: pain, burning, dryness — hydrate or stop
7. Route to @behlau if worsening after 3 days
