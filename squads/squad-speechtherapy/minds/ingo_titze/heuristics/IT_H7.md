# Heuristic: Pediatric or Elderly Voice

- **ID:** IT_H7
- **Name:** Pediatric or Elderly Voice
- **Agent:** ingo-titze
- **Severity:** HIGH

## When
Patient is under 18 or over 65.

## Rule
Adjust SOVT intensity DOWN by 30%.
Use shorter sessions (3 min max per exercise).
For elderly: watch for cardiovascular contraindications with straw resistance.
Route to @behlau for clinical clearance before initiating any protocol.

## Rationale
Vocal fold tissue properties change significantly across lifespan. Generic dosing is inappropriate.

## Action
1. Reduce SOVT intensity by 30% from standard adult protocol
2. Shorten sessions: max 3 min per exercise
3. For elderly: screen for cardiovascular contraindications before WRSP
4. Route to @behlau for clinical clearance before starting
5. Monitor more frequently (weekly rather than biweekly)
