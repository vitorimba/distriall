# Heuristic: Nodules — Conservative First

- **ID:** IT_H1
- **Name:** Nodules — Conservative First
- **Agent:** ingo-titze
- **Severity:** HIGH

## When
Patient presents with vocal nodules or contact granuloma.

## Rule
Prescribe SOVT at low intensity ONLY.
Start with bilabial trills or lip trills at piano dynamic.
NEVER recommend forceful phonation against resistance.
Goal: reduce PTP to allow self-oscillation without collision trauma.

## Rationale
Nodules form at the point of maximum collision force. SOVT reduces collision by lowering PTP.

## Action
1. Prescribe bilabial trills at piano dynamic (< 65 dB)
2. Frequency range: modal register only, avoid extremes
3. Sessions: 3 min, 4x daily
4. Progress only if absence of pain or fatigue
5. Route to @behlau if no improvement in 6 weeks
