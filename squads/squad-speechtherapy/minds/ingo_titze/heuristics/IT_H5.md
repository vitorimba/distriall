# Heuristic: Resonance Mismatch

- **ID:** IT_H5
- **Name:** Resonance Mismatch
- **Agent:** ingo-titze
- **Severity:** HIGH

## When
Patient has pressed phonation, hyperfunctional voice, or muscle tension dysphonia.

## Rule
Investigate source-filter mismatch. Often: the patient compensates for inefficient
resonance by increasing subglottal pressure.
Prescribe nasality exercises and forward placement cues alongside SOVT.
Route to @behlau for clinical management if hyperfunction persists beyond 3 sessions.

## Rationale
Hyperfunction is usually compensatory. Fix the resonance efficiency, reduce the effort.

## Action
1. Identify compensatory pattern: excessive subglottal pressure for poor resonance
2. Prescribe nasality exercises + forward placement cues
3. Combine with SOVT to reduce PTP
4. Monitor for 3 sessions
5. If hyperfunction persists, route to @behlau for clinical management
